01

Browse CISCE resources by class and subject

CISCE schools cover a broad range of subjects, and the books used can vary by class and school. StudyWudy therefore starts with the learner's class and subject before showing the available textbooks, chapters and questions.

This avoids mixing material from different standards and makes it easier to return to the same book during homework, revision or exam preparation.

02

ICSE solutions for secondary-level preparation

The Indian Certificate of Secondary Education is the Class X examination under CISCE. Students can use the available Class 9 and Class 10 pathways to revise subject concepts, locate textbook questions and study clear answers while keeping the correct chapter context visible.

For the current ICSE regulations and subject syllabuses, refer to the official CISCE ICSE publication.

03

ISC answers for senior-secondary subjects

The Indian School Certificate is the Class XII examination under CISCE. The Class 12 pathway groups the senior-secondary subjects and available books so students can focus on the terminology, depth and answer format required by their own course.

Students and teachers should confirm current requirements through the official CISCE ISC regulations.

04

How to study with CISCE answers

  1. Select your CISCE class above.
  2. Choose the subject and the textbook prescribed by your school.
  3. Open the matching chapter and exercise question.
  4. Use the explanation to improve your own written answer.

Because schools may prescribe different books, confirm the title and chapter before relying on a solution for revision.

Independent study support

StudyWudy is an independent study resource and is not affiliated with CISCE. Official regulations, syllabuses, notices and examination information should always be verified on the CISCE website.

Frequently asked questions

CISCE, ICSE and ISC solutions: common questions

What is the difference between ICSE and ISC material?+

ICSE refers to the Class X examination, while ISC refers to the Class XII examination. Select the correct class above to keep subjects and study material in the right pathway.

How do I find CISCE textbook solutions?+

Choose your class, subject and the textbook prescribed by your school, then open its chapter list to find available questions and answers.

Why do textbook choices vary across CISCE schools?+

CISCE publishes regulations and syllabuses, while schools may prescribe different books for some classes and subjects. Always match the book title before opening a solution.

Where can I check the latest ICSE or ISC syllabus?+

Use the official CISCE website and its current regulations and syllabuses for authoritative course and examination information.
