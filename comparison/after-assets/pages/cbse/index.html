CBSE solutions and NCERT textbook answers by class

01

Find CBSE study material from Class 1 to Class 12

The class selector above is the starting point for every available CBSE resource. Each class opens its own subject collection, so primary, secondary and senior-secondary material stays separated and easy to revisit.

Inside a subject, follow the textbook and chapter sequence to reach exercise questions, worked solutions and revision material without searching through an unrelated content feed.

02

NCERT textbook answers in chapter order

NCERT publishes model textbooks and learning material used widely in CBSE classrooms. StudyWudy organises the available answers around the correct book and chapter, with short responses, full explanations, tables, diagrams and step-by-step numericals presented according to the question type.

For authorised textbook PDFs for Classes I to XII, use the official NCERT textbook portal.

03

Prepare for CBSE secondary and senior secondary

Students in Classes 9 and 10 can concentrate on the relevant secondary subjects, while Classes 11 and 12 can move directly into their senior-secondary course pathways. Studying in textbook order helps connect individual answers to the wider unit instead of treating every question as an isolated fact.

Always verify the current syllabus, subject structure and official academic notices on CBSE Academic.

04

How to use CBSE solutions effectively

  1. Select your CBSE class above.
  2. Open the subject and the NCERT book used in school.
  3. Choose the chapter, then locate the exact exercise question.
  4. Study the method and attempt the question again independently.

Use each solution as a learning aid: compare your attempt, identify the missing step and return to the chapter concept when the reasoning is unclear.

Independent study support

StudyWudy is an independent learning resource and is not the official website of CBSE or NCERT. Official textbooks, syllabuses, circulars and examination updates should be checked on their respective websites.

Frequently asked questions

CBSE and NCERT solutions: common questions

Where can I find CBSE solutions by class?+

Select a class at the top of this page, then choose the available subject, textbook and chapter. Every solution remains inside that class-specific path.

Does StudyWudy provide NCERT textbook answers?+

StudyWudy organises available textbook solutions by book, chapter and question. Use NCERT's official portal for authorised textbook PDFs.

Are Class 10 and Class 12 CBSE resources included?+

Class 10 and Class 12 pathways are available in the class selector. The exact subjects, books and questions shown depend on the material currently published in the catalogue.

Where should I check the latest CBSE syllabus?+

Check CBSE Academic for the current curriculum and official academic notices, because syllabus details can change by academic session.
