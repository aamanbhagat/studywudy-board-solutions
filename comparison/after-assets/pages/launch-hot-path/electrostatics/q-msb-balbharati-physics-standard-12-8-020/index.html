In a parallel plate capacitor with air between the plates, each plate has an area of 6 x 10^(−3) m^(2) and the separation between the plates is 2 mm.

  1. Calculate the capacitance of the capacitor.
  1. If this capacitor is connected to 100 V supply, what would be the charge on each plate?
  1. How would charge on the plates be affected if a 2 mm thick mica sheet of k = 6 is inserted between the plates while the voltage supply remains connected?

Step-by-step solution

Step-by-step solution

  1. Step 1

    Given quantities

    Area of each plate: A = 6 × 10⁻³ m²; separation: d = 2mm = 2 × 10⁻³ m; permittivity of free space: ε₀ = 8.85 × 10⁻¹² C² N⁻¹ m⁻²; supply voltage: V = 100 V; dielectric constant of mica: k = 6.

  2. Step 2

    Formula – Capacitance of parallel-plate capacitor (air)

    The capacitance with air (vacuum) between the plates is given by the standard parallel-plate formula.

    C=ε0Ad

  3. Step 3

    Substitution and calculation – Part 1

    Substitute the known values into the capacitance formula.

    C=8.85×10−12×6×10−32×10−3=53.1×10−152×10−3=26.55×10−12F≈26.55pF

  4. Step 4

    Formula – Charge on plates

    The charge stored on each plate of a capacitor connected to a voltage V is Q = CV.

    Q=CV

  5. Step 5

    Substitution and calculation – Part 2

    Substitute C = 26.55 × 10⁻¹² F and V = 100 V.

    Q=26.55×10−12×100=2655×10−12C=2.655×10−9C≈2.655nC

  6. Step 6

    New capacitance with mica dielectric – Part 3

    Inserting a dielectric of dielectric constant k between the plates multiplies the capacitance by k. The voltage supply remains at 100 V, so V is unchanged.

    C'=kC=6×26.55×10−12=159.3×10−12F=159.3pF

  7. Step 7

    New charge with mica – Part 3

    With the supply still connected, the new charge is Q' = C'V. The charge increases by the same factor k = 6 compared with the air-capacitor case.

    Q'=C'V=159.3×10−12×100=1.593×10−8C≈15.93nC

Final answer

  1. Capacitance (air): C = 26.55 pF ≈ 26.55 × 10⁻¹² F
  2. Charge at 100 V: Q = 2.655 × 10⁻⁹ C ≈ 2.655 nC
  3. With 2mm mica (k = 6) inserted while supply remains connected: new capacitance C′ = 159.3 pF and new charge Q′ = 1.593 × 10⁻⁸ C ≈ 15.93 nC. The charge increases by a factor of k = 6 (from ≈ 2.655 nC to ≈ 15.93 nC).
