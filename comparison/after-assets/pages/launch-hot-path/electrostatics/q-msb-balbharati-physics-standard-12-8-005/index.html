A parallel-plate capacitor has circular plates of radius 8 cm and plate separation 1 mm. What will be the charge on the plates if a potential difference of 100 V is applied across its plates?

  1. a1.78 × 10−8C✓ Correct
  2. b1.78 × 10−5C
  3. c4.3 × 104C
  4. d2 × 10−9C
Main solution
  • ✓ Method explained
  • ✓ Formula or principle identified
  • ✓ Diagram included
  • ✓ Final answer separated

Answer and explanation

The correct answer is 1.78 × 10−8C.

Why this is correct

The capacitance of a parallel-plate capacitor is C=ε0Ad, and the charge is Q=CV.

With radius r=8cm=0.08m, plate separation d=1mm=10−3m, and V=100V:

A=πr2=π×(0.08)2≈0.02011m2

C=8.85×10−12×0.0201110−3≈1.78×10−10F

Q=CV=1.78×10−10×100=1.78×10−8C

Related concept

Formula or principle used

The capacitance of a parallel-plate capacitor is C=ε0Ad, and the charge is Q=CV.
