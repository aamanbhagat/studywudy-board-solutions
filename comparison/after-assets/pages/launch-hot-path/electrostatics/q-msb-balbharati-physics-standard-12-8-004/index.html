Question 4 · Single-choice MCQ

Question

Choose the correct:

Charge + q and - q are placed at points A and B respectively which are distance 2L apart. C is the midpoint of A and B. The work done in moving a charge + Q from C to D, along the semicircle CD as shown in the figure below, is __.

  1. A(-qQ)/(6piε_0 L)Correct option
  2. B(qQ)/(2piε_0 L)
  3. C(qQ)/(6piε_0 L)
  4. D(-qQ)/(4piε_0 L)
Solution

Answer and explanation

Main solution
  • ✓ Method explained
  • ✓ Formula or principle identified
  • ✓ Final answer separated

Explanation

The work done equals Q(VD−VC). Point C is the midpoint of AB, so the potential there due to +q (distance L) and −q (distance L) is zero. Point D is on the line AB extended beyond B by L, so AD=3L and BD=L. Thus VD=kq3L+k(−q)L=kq3L−kqL=−2kq3L. Therefore W=Q(−2kq3L−0)=−2kqQ3L=−qQ6πε0L.
