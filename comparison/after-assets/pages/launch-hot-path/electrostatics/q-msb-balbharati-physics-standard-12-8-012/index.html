Question 12 · Brief answer

Question

Three charges –q, +Q, and –q are placed at equal distances on a straight line. If the potential energy of the system of the three charges is zero, then what is the ratio of Q: q?

Answer

Q : q = 1 : 4

Setup:
Place the charges on a straight line with equal separation a:
−q (+Q) −q
underbrace a}underbrace a}

Potential energy of each pair:

U12=k(−q)(+Q)a=−kqQa

U23=k(+Q)(−q)a=−kqQa

U13=k(−q)(−q)2a=+kq22a

Total potential energy:
U=U12+U23+U13=−2kqQa+kq22a

Setting U=0:
−2kqQa+kq22a=0

Multiply through by ak:
−2qQ+q22=0

2qQ=q22

Q=q4

Qq=14

Step-by-step solution

  1. Step 1

    Identify the three pairs

    With charges –q at position 0, +Q at position a, and –q at position 2a, there are three interacting pairs: (–q, +Q) at distance a, (+Q, –q) at distance a, and (–q, –q) at distance 2a.

  2. Step 2

    Write potential energy for each pair

    Using U = kq₁q₂/r for each pair:
    U₁₂ = k(–q)+Qa = –kqQ/a
    U₂₃ = k(+Q)−qa = –kqQ/a
    U₁₃ = k(–q)−q2a = +kq²/(2a)

    U12=−kqQa, U23=−kqQa, U13=+kq22a

  3. Step 3

    Sum to zero

    Total U = –2kqQ/a + kq²/(2a) = 0. Multiplying through by a/k gives –2qQ + q²/2 = 0, so Q = q/4.

    −2qQ+q22=0⇒Q=q4

Final answer

Q : q = 1 : 4
