Choose the correct option:

Energy stored in a capacitor and dissipated during charging a capacitor bear a ratio.

  1. A1 : 1Correct option
  2. B1 : 2
  3. C2 : 1
  4. D1 : 3

  • ✓ Final answer separated

Step-by-step solution

Explanation

When charging a capacitor through a resistor, the energy supplied by the battery is QV=CV2. Half of this is stored in the capacitor (12CV2) and half is dissipated as heat in the resistor (12CV2). Thus the ratio of energy stored to energy dissipated is 1:1.

Related concept

Formula or principle used

QV=CV2
