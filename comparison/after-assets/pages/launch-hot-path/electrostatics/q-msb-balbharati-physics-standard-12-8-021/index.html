Question 21 · Numerical

Question

Find the equivalent capacitance between P and Q in the following diagram. The area of each plate is A and the separation between plates is d.

Step-by-step solution

  1. Step 1

    Given (Diagram 1)

    Three dielectric slabs of equal thickness d/3 and area A are placed between plates P and Q with dielectric constants k₁ = 1, k₂ = 3, k₃ = 6. Each slab forms an individual capacitor and all three are in series (field traverses them sequentially).

  2. Step 2

    Capacitance of each slab (Diagram 1)

    For a parallel-plate capacitor with dielectric constant k, area A, and separation t: C = kε₀A/t. Here t = d/3 for each slab.

    Ci=kiε0Ad/3=3kiε0Ad

  3. Step 3

    Individual capacitances (Diagram 1)

    Substituting k₁ = 1, k₂ = 3, k₃ = 6:

    C1=3ε0Ad, C2=9ε0Ad, C3=18ε0Ad

  4. Step 4

    Series formula (Diagram 1)

    For capacitors in series: 1/Ceq = 1/C₁ + 1/C₂ + 1/C₃

    1Ceq,1=d3ε0A+d9ε0A+d18ε0A=dε0A·6+2+118=d2ε0A

  5. Step 5

    Result (Diagram 1)

    Taking the reciprocal:

    Ceq,1=2ε0Ad

  6. Step 6

    Given (Diagram 2)

    The capacitor of total area A and separation d is divided: left half (area A/2, separation d) has k₁ = 4; right half is split horizontally — top (area A/2, separation d/2) has k₂ = 3; bottom (area A/2, separation d/2) has k₃ = 6. The right two dielectrics are in series with each other; that combination is in parallel with the left dielectric.

  7. Step 7

    Capacitance of left slab (Diagram 2)

    Left section occupies half the area and the full gap d.

    C1=4ε0(A/2)d=2ε0Ad

  8. Step 8

    Capacitances of right slabs (Diagram 2)

    Each right slab occupies half the area and half the gap.

    C2=3ε0(A/2)d/2=3ε0Ad, C3=6ε0(A/2)d/2=6ε0Ad

  9. Step 9

    Series combination of right slabs (Diagram 2)

    C₂ and C₃ are in series (field passes through both along the same path on the right half).

    1C23=d3ε0A+d6ε0A=dε0A·12⇒C23=2ε0Ad

  10. Step 10

    Parallel combination (Diagram 2)

    C₁ and C₂₃ are connected between the same plates P and Q, hence in parallel.

    Ceq,2=C1+C23=2ε0Ad+2ε0Ad=4ε0Ad

Final answer

Diagram 1 (three slabs in series, k₁=1, k₂=3, k₃=6, each thickness d/3): Ceq = 2ε₀A/d. Diagram 2 (left: k₁=4 full gap; right-top: k₂=3, right-bottom: k₃=6, each half-gap, both half-area): Ceq = 4ε₀A/d.
