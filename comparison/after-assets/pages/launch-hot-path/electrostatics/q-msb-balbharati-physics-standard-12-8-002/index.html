A slab of material of dielectric constant k has the same area A as the plates of a parallel plate capacitor and has a thickness (3/4d), where d is the separation of the plates. The change in capacitance when the slab is inserted between the plates is ______.

  1. AC = (Aε_0)/d ((k + 3)/(4k))
  2. BC = (Aε_0)/d ((2k)/(k + 3))
  3. CC = (Aε_0)/d ((k + 3)/(2k))
  4. DC = (Aε_0)/d ((4k)/(k + 3))Correct option

Solution

When a dielectric slab of thickness t=34d is inserted, the air gap remaining is d−34d=d4. The system acts as two capacitors in series:

C1=ε0Ad/4=4ε0Ad,C2=kε0A3d/4=4kε0A3d

1C=1C1+1C2=d4ε0A+3d4kε0A=d4ε0A(1+3k)=d4ε0A·k+3k

C=ε0Ad·4kk+3
