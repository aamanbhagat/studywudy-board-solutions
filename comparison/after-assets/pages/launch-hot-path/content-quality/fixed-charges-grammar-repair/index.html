Step-by-step solution

Setting up the geometry

Place the origin at the midpoint. The two fixed charges −q are at (−d,0) and (+d,0). The mobile charge +q (mass m) is displaced to (0,x) with xd.

Step 1 – Distance from each fixed charge to the displaced position

By Pythagoras, each fixed charge is at distance r=√(d2+x2) from the displaced position of +q.

Step 2 – Coulomb force magnitude from each −q

Since the interaction is attractive: F=14π0·q2d2+x2

Step 3 – Resolving forces; net restoring force

By symmetry the horizontal (along the original line) components of the two forces cancel. Each force has a vertical component (anti-parallel to x): θ=(x)/(√(d2+x2)) The total restoring force is Fnet=−2Fθ=−2q24π0·(x)/((d2+x2)3/2)

Step 4 – Small-displacement approximation (xd)

(d2+x2)3/2≈d3 Fnet≈−q22π0d3x

This is of the form F=−kx with k=q22π0d3, confirming SHM.

Step 5 – Angular frequency

Using Newton's second law for SHM, Fnet=−mω2x: mω2=q22π0d3ω=√(q22π0md3)

Step 6 – Time period

T=2πω=2π√(2π0md3q2)

Squaring: T2=4π2·(2π0md3/q2)=(8πε03md3/q2)
