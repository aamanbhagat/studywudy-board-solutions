Step-by-step solution

Let the non-conducting plate occupy −d/2≤z≤d/2, with its central plane at z=0. By symmetry, the electric field inside is perpendicular to the plate and depends only on the distance from the central plane. For a point on the positive side at distance x, where 0<x<d/2, the field is Equation review pending where n is directed away from the central plane. Its magnitude is therefore E=ρx/0. The graph is a straight line through the origin. Since the stated interval 0<x<d extends beyond the physical interior when d denotes the total thickness, the field is linear only up to x=d/2; outside the plate it remains constant.
