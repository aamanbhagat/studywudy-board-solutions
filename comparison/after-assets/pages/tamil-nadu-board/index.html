01

Find Tamil Nadu Board material by standard

Every class has its own subject collection, helping students avoid mixing chapters from different standards. From the class page, open the relevant subject, select the textbook and continue to the chapter you are studying.

The consistent class-to-question path supports daily lessons, homework checks, chapter revision and exam preparation in both English- and Tamil-medium pathways where material is available.

02

Samacheer Kalvi answers in textbook order

Tamil Nadu textbooks are published through the state's Textbook and Educational Services Corporation. StudyWudy provides independent learning support around the available books, including concise definitions, worked answers, comparisons, tables, diagrams and calculations matched to each question.

For official textbook information and authorised books, visit the Tamil Nadu Textbook and Educational Services Corporation.

03

Prepare for Tamil Nadu SSLC and HSE subjects

Tamil Nadu's Directorate of Government Examinations conducts the Class 10 SSLC and Class 12 Higher Secondary examinations. Students can use the available Class 10, 11 and 12 subject pathways to revisit chapters, practise textbook questions and strengthen written answers.

Confirm examination notices, timetables, results and official question-paper resources through the Tamil Nadu Directorate of Government Examinations.

04

How to use Samacheer Kalvi solutions

  1. Select your Tamil Nadu Board class above.
  2. Choose the subject and language-medium textbook used in school.
  3. Open the matching chapter and locate the exercise question.
  4. Study the explanation, then write the answer again in your own words.

For numericals and diagrams, repeat the full method yourself so the solution becomes revision practice rather than a line to memorise.

Independent study support

StudyWudy is an independent learning resource and is not an official Tamil Nadu Government, DGE or Textbook Corporation website. Use official portals for authorised books and examination announcements.
