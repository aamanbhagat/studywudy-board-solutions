Question

A parallel-plate capacitor is charged and isolated. What happens to charge, potential and capacitance when the plate separation increases?

01
Use the condition

The isolated capacitor has no path for charge to leave.

Q remains constant
02
Apply the relations

Since C = εA/d and V = Q/C, increasing d lowers C and raises V.

Final answerCharge unchanged; potential increases; capacitance decreases.
✓
★

Remember: isolation fixes charge, while geometry changes capacitance.
